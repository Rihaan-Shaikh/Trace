# NOVAMART CORPORATE PRICING POLICY
# Document ID: POL-2025-PRICING-CORP
# Category: Pricing Policy
# Effective Date: January 15, 2025

Section 1 - Objective:
To establish baseline wholesale pricing rules, floor margins, and executive approval requirements for commercial discounts across all product lines.

Section 2 - Gross Margin Floor:
The standard target gross margin for all catalog items is 25.0%. No sales representative or account executive may quote pricing yielding an account-level gross margin below 12.0% without written approval from the Chief Commercial Officer.

Section 3 - Contract vs Non-Contract Accounts:
Enterprise accounts governed by active bilateral Master Services Agreements (MSAs) are subject to contractual terms that supersede discretionary pricing guidelines. Standard non-contracted SMB and Mid-Market accounts are subject to discretionary review and quarterly margin optimization.
