# COMMERCIAL SALES MEMORANDUM: DISCRETIONARY DISCOUNT GUIDELINES
# Document ID: MEMO-2025-DISC-GUIDELINES
# Category: Discount Guidelines Memo
# To: Regional Sales Directors, Account Executives
# From: VP Commercial Operations

1. Policy Context:
Analysis of Q3/Q4 transaction data indicates significant margin dilution attributable to discretionary promotional discounts exceeding 15.0% on standard commodity SKUs.

2. Discretionary Account Action Authority:
For non-contracted SMB and Mid-Market accounts where account-level gross margin has fallen below 15.0% for two consecutive operating quarters, sales leadership has discretionary authority to terminate or rescale discounts back to the standard catalog grid upon thirty (30) days notice.

3. Protection of Contracted Enterprise Clients:
Under no circumstances should sales representatives unilaterally adjust discounts on Enterprise accounts with active signed MSAs. Contractual remedies, cure periods, and liquidated damages clauses apply to these accounts.
